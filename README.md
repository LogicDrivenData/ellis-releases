# Ellis

Ellis runs your business from one place: tasks, time tracking, clients and CRM, quotes, invoices, contracts, expenses,
mileage, SEO reports and more, with an optional AI assistant. It's a one-time purchase that runs on your own computer,
so your data stays with you.

This page holds the installers only. **A license key is required**: you get it when you buy Ellis
([see the packages](https://buildmycompanyai.com/pricing)).

## Download

Open **[Releases](https://github.com/LogicDrivenData/ellis-releases/releases/latest)** and download the file for your
computer:

| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 or 11 | `Ellis-Setup-<version>.exe` |
| Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) | `Ellis-<version>.dmg` |

## Install

**Windows**
1. Run `Ellis-Setup-<version>.exe`. No administrator password is needed.
2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. (This goes away once our
   Windows signing certificate is issued.)
3. Ellis lives in the tray by the clock: right-click its icon for **Open Ellis** or **Quit**.

**Mac**
1. Open the `.dmg` and drag **Ellis** onto **Applications**.
2. Open Ellis from Applications. It's signed and notarized by Apple (from version 0.3.3), so it opens normally.
3. Ellis lives in the menu bar at the top of the screen.

The first time it opens, Ellis walks you through setup in your browser and asks for your license key. You can also
paste the key later in **Settings → General → Your plan**.

## Updating

When a new version is out, Ellis shows a banner at the top of the app. To update:
1. **Quit** Ellis (tray icon on Windows, menu bar icon on Mac).
2. Install the new version the same way: run the new installer on Windows, or drag the new Ellis into Applications and
   choose **Replace** on a Mac.

Your data, settings and license key are kept: they live in your user folder, not with the program, so updating or
reinstalling never touches them. New versions are included while your support plan is active. Without one, Ellis keeps
working on the version you have.
